PodOuvir is a simple, robust podcast player.

* Search and subscribe to podcasts, or add any RSS feed
* Organise your library by genre and sort it your way
* Play, pause, stop, skip and listen at 1x, 1.25x, 1.5x or 2x
* Reliable background playback with lock-screen and headset controls
* Resumes every episode where you left off
* No accounts, no tracking
